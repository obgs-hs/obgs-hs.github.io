OBGS

Open Board Game Sheets — A suite of Haskell packages for declaratively describing board game score sheets and evaluating their rules with static type safety.

v0.1.0.0

Type families and first-class families for higher-order type-level computations, promoted numerical arithmetic with overflow semantics, finite inhabitants, and structured compile-time error diagnostics.
